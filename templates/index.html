The problem we're solving

Most students find out they are struggling only when the results arrive. By then it is too late to change anything.

🙈 Students are guessing

"Am I studying enough? Is my attendance hurting me?" Most students have no clear way to connect their habits to their likely result.

👥 Teachers can't watch everyone

With dozens of students in a class, a teacher cannot notice every student who is slipping before the exam.

⏰ Help comes too late

Support after a failed exam is far less useful than support given weeks earlier. StudyPulse gives an early warning so help can come in time.
